Loose Park

Level 2 · Completion
Scarecrow→Award→Outstanding in his field
1
Place

Loose Park's rose garden

Named for Jacob Loose of the Sunshine Biscuit company, the park’s formal rose garden holds nearly 3,000 roses of about 130 varieties, and was honored in 1989 by All-America Rose Selections as an outstanding public rose garden.

2
Joke

The ceremony

A small, entirely sincere award presentation — ribbon, medal, applause. Nobody in the field treats it as anything but an ordinary Tuesday.
